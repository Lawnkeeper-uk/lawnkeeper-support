Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

In summary: LawnKeeper UK is a free UK lawn-care companion. We use information you provide to deliver lawn-care tools, save your preferences and operate the community. You enter your postcode manually for local weather and watering guidance. We do not use advertising or advertising-tracking services.

1. Who is responsible for your information?

LawnKeeper UK is operated by Darren Bentham ("we", "us" or "our"). For privacy questions or requests, contact UKlawnkeeper@gmail.com.

2. Information we process

  • Account information: email address, account identifier, sign-in credentials handled by our authentication provider, and any display name you choose.
  • Community information: posts, comments, photographs, likes, reports and other content you submit. Posts and display names may be visible to other users.
  • Lawn and app preferences: lawn size, lawn-care selections, task progress, watering settings, assessments and other information you enter. Some settings may be saved on your device; account-related information may be stored through our backend.
  • Postcode and weather: a postcode you enter to provide relevant local weather and watering guidance. We do not require your device's GPS location for this feature.
  • Technical information: information necessary to run and secure the service, which may include IP addresses, request details, authentication events and error or security logs handled by our hosting and service providers.
  • Support messages: your email address and anything you choose to send us when requesting assistance.

3. Why we use information and our legal bases

We use account details and relevant preferences to provide requested app features and account services (performance of our agreement with you). We use community content to publish and manage posts and replies you choose to share (providing the service you request). We may process reports, security logs and related information to prevent abuse, protect users and maintain the service (our legitimate interests in operating a safe, reliable app). We use support messages to respond to requests (our legitimate interests in providing support, or to meet a legal obligation where applicable). Where a particular activity requires consent, we will seek it and you may withdraw it.

4. Who can see or receive information?

Community posts, uploaded community photographs, comments and your display name may be visible to other users. Avoid posting personal or sensitive information you do not want to share publicly.

We use Supabase to support account authentication, database functions and file storage. Relevant information may also be processed by weather-data services used to deliver postcode-based forecasts and by infrastructure providers needed to operate the app. We do not sell personal information or share it with advertising networks.

Service providers may process information in other countries. Where international transfers of personal information occur, we aim to use appropriate protections required by applicable UK data protection law.

5. How long we keep information

We keep account and associated service information while your account is active and as needed to provide the app. When an account is deleted, associated personal data and user-generated content should be removed unless a limited retention is necessary for legal, security or dispute-resolution reasons. Some records may remain temporarily in protected backups until they expire under the relevant provider's backup lifecycle. Support correspondence and security records are kept only as long as reasonably necessary for their purpose or applicable legal obligations.

6. Your choices and rights

You can choose whether to create an account and what to post in the community. You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information. Depending on the circumstances, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, receive a portable copy of certain information, and withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis.

To exercise these rights or request account deletion, email UKlawnkeeper@gmail.com. Where available, you can also use the account-deletion option within the app. We may need to verify that a request relates to your account before acting on it.

You can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk if you are concerned about how your information is handled.

7. Children

LawnKeeper UK is intended as a general lawn-care service. If you believe a child has shared personal information inappropriately, please contact us so we can review the concern.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect information. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when app features, service providers or legal requirements change. The date at the top of this page will show the latest revision. Material changes will be communicated where required.
