Frequently asked questions

Is LawnKeeper UK free?

Yes. LawnKeeper UK is a free lawn-care companion app.

Do I need an account?

Many lawn-care tools can be used without signing in. An account is required to use community features.

How do watering recommendations work?

The app uses lawn settings and available weather information to help estimate watering needs. Always check actual soil conditions and local water restrictions.

Can I share lawn progress photos?

The app includes lawn progress features and community photo sharing. Only share photos you have permission to use, and avoid including personal information.

What should I include in a bug report?

Tell us what happened, which screen you were using, your iPhone model and iOS version, and any steps that reproduce the problem. Please don't send passwords.

Lawn-care guidance is general information, not a guarantee of results. Local weather, grass type, soil and seasonal conditions vary.
